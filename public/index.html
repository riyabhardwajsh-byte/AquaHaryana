17 Essential Water-Saving Practices

AquaHaryana natively implements and maps all 17 critical water-saving methods optimized for Haryana's fields, homes, and community networks.

Agriculture Practice 01

Drip & Sprinkler Irrigation

Minimizes evaporation losses by releasing water directly onto plant roots through a complex network of pipe nozzles.

Saves 40% - 50% Water Up to 85% Subsidized
Domestic Practice 02

Rooftop Rainwater Harvesting

Captures, channels, and filters precipitation directly from roofs to replenish underground sumps or neighborhood aquifers.

Saves 30% Domestic Water Govt Mandated
Agriculture Practice 03

Crop Diversification

Transitioning high water-demand fields like paddy into cotton, maize, millets, or pulses that flourish in dry climates.

Saves 60% Water ₹7,000/Acre Reward
Agriculture Practice 04

Direct Seeded Rice (DSR)

Sowing seeds directly using specialized seeders, eliminating the water-submerged puddling phase entirely.

Saves 15% - 20% Water ₹4,000/Acre Grant
Agriculture Practice 05

Laser Land Leveling

Uses high-precision laser guidance arrays to level soil flat, preventing dry patches and soggy, flooded corners.

Saves 15% - 20% Water Improves Yield 15%
Agriculture Practice 06

Organic Soil Mulching

Spreading clean organic plant straw, crop husks, or biodegradable sheets over topsoil to block heat and retain dampness.

Saves 25% Evaporation Controls Weeds
Agriculture Practice 07

Zero Tillage Cultivation

Direct sowing into residual crops immediately without plowing, preserving soil moisture and carbon depth.

Saves 15% Irrigation ₹1,000/Acre Scheme
Domestic Practice 08

Greywater Treatment

Filtering and recycling residential sink, basin, and laundry runoff for washing, landscaping, or toilet flushing.

Saves 40% Domestic Use Decentralized Ponds
Agriculture Practice 09

Smart IoT Irrigation

Deploys soil hygrometers and remote microcontrollers to supply water strictly when target moisture limits drop.

Saves 30% - 45% Water Zero Over-Irrigation
Agriculture Practice 10

On-Farm Lined Ponds

Carves clay or high-durability plastic-lined ponds to store rainfall runoff for supplemental field irrigation.

Saves 30% Extraction 70% State Assistance
Agriculture Practice 11

System of Rice Intensification (SRI)

A dynamic method of planting wide-spaced young rice seedlings instead of continuous deep flooding.

Saves 25% - 35% Water 90% Fewer Seeds
Agriculture Practice 12

Hydroponics & Soilless Cultivation

Growing high-yield crops and exotic greens in recirculated water tables inside smart covered polyhouses.

Saves up to 90% Water Up to 65% Polyhouse Subsidy
Agriculture Practice 13

Happy Seeder Technology

Sows wheat directly through heavy paddy stubble mulching, retaining moisture and stopping destructive stubble burning.

Conserves Residual Dampness Subsidized Implements
Community Practice 14

Artificial Borewell Recharge

Diverts surface runoff and stormwater into filter chambers to inject fresh water back into depleted deep aquifers.

Prevents Aquifer Dryness Atal Bhujal Scheme
Agriculture Practice 15

Alternate Wetting & Drying (AWD)

Flooding rice fields only after soil dry cycles, reducing methane emissions and water waste.

Saves 20% - 25% Water Healthy Roots
Agriculture Practice 16

Sub-surface Drip Irrigation

Buries drip emitter pipes 15-30 cm below surface clay to nourish roots directly without topsoil runoff or evaporation.

Saves 50% - 60% Irrigation Highly Recommended
Community Practice 17

Community Water Budgeting

Panchayat-led water audits measuring community supply vs. agricultural cropping targets to draft balanced consumption security plans.

Saves 15% Village Water Cooperative Action

Haryana Policy Support

Supported Government Schemes

Haryana is pioneer in incentivizing farmers to safeguard state water resources. Here are key schemes backed inside our app:

Mera Pani - Meri Virasat

Offers financial incentive of **₹7,000 per acre** to farmers transitioning away from water-submerged paddy to crops like maize, cotton, and pulses in critical overdraft zones.

MICADA Micro-Irrigation

The Canal Area Development Authority offers up to **85% subsidy** for installing drip and sprinkler arrays in fields to encourage high efficiency micro water application.

Atal Bhujal Yojana

Empowers local community water security layouts, mapping and measuring aquifer health under village-level Water Budgeting to prevent excessive tubewell extraction.

Installing AquaHaryana Native App

Follow these basic, straightforward steps to experience the completed Android app in real-time on your smartphone or test system.

1

Run or Preview on AI Studio Platform

The easiest and most immediate way to view the beautiful Compose interface, interact with the groundwater risk map, favorites list, or chat with the actual Gemini-powered AquaBot is inside Google AI Studio's sidebar preview screen.

2

Download or Export Android Package (APK/AAB)

Click on the **Settings/Menu icon** on the top right header of the Google AI Studio interface. Select **"Export/Download"** options. You can easily bundle the source code as a ZIP archive, sync direct to GitHub, or build and fetch the signed debugging **APK** for direct loading onto your Android phone!

3

Vercel Deployment Showcase

Since Vercel hosts web files and does not directly execute or compile Gradle Android structures, we have built this rich HTML5 showcase page using standard responsive styling. Anyone arriving at your Vercel URL can read this guide, examine water practices, and learn how to launch your app.
